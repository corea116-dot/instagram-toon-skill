# Brand Bible

## Promise

Create compact six-beat stories delivered as three images: one strong opening hook, one four-panel development composite, and one gentle, clear ending payoff. Humor should invite recognition rather than ridicule.

## Voice

- Warm, observant, concise, and lightly self-deprecating
- Specific everyday details over generic life advice
- Character-first humor over slogans or memes
- No moral lecture after the punch line

## Visual identity

- Canvas: 1080x1350 pixels
- Composition: clean mobile-first silhouettes, restrained backgrounds, visible expressions
- Palette: warm neutrals with one controlled accent color per scene
- Typography: Korean text is composited after art generation; never ask an image model to render it
- Branding: no platform logo, watermark, or signature unless the user later supplies and approves an asset

## Editorial boundaries

Keep every episode in draft status until the user reviews it. Do not publish, upload, schedule, or contact an external service without explicit approval. Use the banned-topic memory for safety checks and the episode history for originality checks.

When `$instagram-toon` is invoked without a topic, select one automatically through EditorialScoutAgent. Favor relatable everyday friction and warm, self-aware humor over novelty for its own sake. Public-web material is only a signal: abstract it into an anonymous observation, retain short source metadata, and never copy a post, personal anecdote, image, identity, or wording. If no safer choice exists, ask instead of forcing a trend.

Humor begins with a concrete human contradiction, never a broad topic container. Compress that contradiction into one hook promise, four visible development changes, and a payoff that reframes the opening. Use a varied engine palette: `semantic_authority_reversal`, `personified_cognition_action_contradiction`, `self_rationalization_loop`, `magnitude_mismatch`, `repetition_escalation`, `collective_optimism_reality_collapse`, `moving_goalpost_paralysis`, or `social_timing_or_role_reversal`. Topicless candidates use at least three engines; Idea directions use at least two. A recent engine match can lower novelty, but matching beat signature or payoff is a hard duplicate.

Keep the edge humane: blunt, mildly sarcastic, or self-directed non-profane Korean is welcome when it serves recognition rather than humiliation. Do not use profanity, disguised profanity, slurs, dehumanizing language, identity-based attacks, or humiliating nicknames.

## Reference assets

Character references belong in `assets/references/characters/`; style references belong in `assets/references/styles/`. Record only project-relative paths in the JSON bibles so the project remains portable.
